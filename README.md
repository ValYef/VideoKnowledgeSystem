# Video Knowledge System 
is a microservice-based intelligent system for automated knowledge extraction from video resources.

The project aims to transform unstructured video content into a structured knowledge base using computer vision, OCR, speech processing, and semantic search technologies.

The system is being developed as part of academic research in the field of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence and serves as the foundation for future research on multimodal video understanding and knowledge retrieval.

### Project Goals
- Extract meaningful information from educational and informational videos.
- Detect and recognize textual content in video frames.
- Classify visual content such as slides, charts, tables, and diagrams.
- Build structured knowledge bases from extracted information.
- Support semantic search across video-derived knowledge.
- Enable future multimodal integration of visual, textual, and speech information.
